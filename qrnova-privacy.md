# QR Nova - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Applies to the Android app "QR Nova" (package com.solvalix.qrnova).

QR Nova is a QR code and barcode scanner and generator. This policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and what is shared with third parties.

## 1. Information we do not collect

QR Nova does not require an account and does not send the content of your scans, your generated codes, your camera images, or your scan history to us or to any server. All code recognition is performed on your device.

## 2. Data stored on your device

**Scan history and favorites:** saved in a local database on your phone so you can find past scans. You can delete single items or the whole history in the app at any time, and you can turn history saving off in Settings.

**Settings:** such as sound, vibration, theme and batch mode.

**Generated QR images:** saved to your Pictures folder only when you tap "Save".

This data is removed when you uninstall the app (unless you exported or saved it elsewhere).

## 3. Permissions

**Camera:** used only to read codes in real time. Images are processed on the device and never stored or transmitted by the app.

**Internet / network state:** used only to load advertisements (see below). Scanning and generating codes work fully offline.

**Vibration:** for haptic feedback after a successful scan.

**Photos:** only the single image you pick (or share with the app) is read, via the system photo picker, to detect a code inside it.

## 4. Link safety check

When a scanned code contains a web link, QR Nova analyses the address on your device to warn you about possible phishing. The address is not sent to any server for this check. Links are opened only when you tap to open them (or when you enable automatic opening of safe links in Settings).

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

QR Nova is free and supported by advertising served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as the advertising ID of your device, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information, and ad interaction data. This data is handled by Google under its own policies: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [AdMob partners](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad preferences & consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

## 6. Children

QR Nova is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Security

Because your scan data never leaves your device, the main safeguard is your device itself. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping Android up to date.

## 8. Your rights

Since the app does not collect personal data on our servers, there is no account data for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising purposes, please use the controls described in section 5 or contact Google directly.
