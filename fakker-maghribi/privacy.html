الإعلانات والتحليلات: هاد النسخة ما فيهاش إعلانات وما فيهاش أدوات تتبّع. إلا زدناهم فالمستقبل غادي نحدّثو هاد الصفحة قبل.

الأطفال: اللعبة ما كتجمعش معلومات شخصية على حتى واحد، بما فيهم الأطفال.

التواصل: عبر الإيميل ديال المطوّر المكتوب فصفحة التطبيق على Google Play.

English

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Fakker Maghribi is a trivia game about Moroccan culture and Darija.

Data we collect: none. The app requires no account and does not send any personal data to our servers.

Data stored on your device: nickname, avatar, selected city, scores and progress. This stays on your phone only and can be deleted from Settings › Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

Local Wi-Fi / hotspot play: when you play with friends on the same local network, the app exchanges only nicknames, avatars and in-game answers directly between the devices. This data does not go through the internet and is not stored by us. The app uses the local network permission for this feature only.

Ads and analytics: this version contains no advertising and no tracking or analytics SDKs. If this changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children: the app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact: via the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
